# Kindling

The language of Kindling: a monthly community vote that picks open-source projects to receive money from a shared pot, which Open Collective holds and pays out.

## Language

### People

**Member**:
Anyone signed in to Kindling with GitHub. A **Member** can nominate projects.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Subscriber**:
A **Member** who has linked an Open Collective profile with a current subscription to the collective. Once money is on, only **Subscribers** vote.
_Avoid_: Backer, donor, supporter

**Operator**:
The person who runs Kindling: approves nominations, removes projects and closes the month.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator

### The vote

**Practice round**:
A month that runs before money is on: every eligible **Member** votes and the month ends with one **Pick** and no **Prize**.
_Avoid_: Practice mode, trial, beta

**Pick**:
A project that won a month's vote. A month has one or more **Picks**, one for each **Prize** the **Pot** covers. A practice month has exactly one **Pick** and no **Prize**.
_Avoid_: Spotlight, winner (in copy), Kindling Pick (as if there were only one)

### Money

**Pot**:
The money available for **Prizes** when a month closes: the collective's balance minus money already owed and the operator's share.
_Avoid_: Fund, balance

**Prize**:
$200 from the **Pot**, paid to one **Pick** through Open Collective.
_Avoid_: Grant, payout
